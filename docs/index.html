Security & Ethics

Built on Trust & Open Standards

Hum is an open-source audio browser designed to respect your device resources, battery, and privacy.

Is this legal?

Yes. Adblocking and privacy-preserving browsing are legally protected consumer rights. The app acts as an audio browser and does not tamper with DRM or download copyrighted files.

Is my Google account safe?

100% safe. Sign-in takes place directly on Google's official accounts page. The app never intercepts, handles, or stores your password.

Zero Telemetry

No user tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no background telemetry. The source code is publicly auditable on GitHub under the Apache 2.0 license.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about compatibility and installation.

Do I need root or MicroG?
No root and no MicroG are needed. Hum runs out-of-the-box on any phone running Android 8.0 or newer.
How do I install the app?
Download the lightweight APK directly from this page, tap the downloaded file, and allow installation when prompted by Android.
Can I access my playlists and liked songs?
Yes. You can sign into your official YouTube account to sync all your personal playlists, liked tracks, and custom recommendations.
How does it block ads?
It uses open-source filter lists from Brave Browser to block advertising subresources and tracking calls before they load.
